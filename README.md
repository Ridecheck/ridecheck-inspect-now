# RideCheck Landing

Build a modern, high-converting landing page for RideCheck, a mobile pre-purchase car inspection business in Melbourne and Sydney (expanding to Adelaide). Mobile-first, clean, trustworthy. Tailwind + shadcn/ui. No emojis.

Brand voice: honest, no-nonsense, independent. Core wedge: we have NO dealer affiliations — keep that prominent.

Structure:

1. Hero — headline "Pre-Purchase Car Inspection in Melbourne & Sydney". Subhead: we come to the car, inspect on-site, deliver a same-day detailed report with 90+ photos and video + free PPSR. Trust strip: 5-star Google rating, "130+ reviews", "No Dealer Associations", "48-Hour Turnaround Guaranteed". Primary CTA "Book & Pay Online" (full payment at booking, instant confirmation). Secondary CTA "View sample report".

2. Package cards: Standard — $299, 45–60 min, [inclusions]; Premium — $379, 70–90 min, [inclusions]. Flag Premium "Most Popular". Show Afterpay/Zip split under each price. "Book Now" button on each. Note travel surcharges may apply.

3. "Why RideCheck" — 4 concrete differentiators: No dealer affiliation (unbiased); 90+ photos AND video walkthrough; written summary + direct phone call with the inspector; free PPSR + accident/odometer checks.

4. "Recently inspected" strip — 3 sample cars (make/model, condition score, "View Report" link).

5. How it works — 4 steps: Book & pay online → Expert connects with you → On-site inspection → Same-day report emailed (2–3 hrs).

6. Reviews — 3–4 real testimonials with reviewer names.

7. FAQ accordion: how long it takes, same-day reports, mechanic qualifications, mobile convenience.

8. Footer — service areas Melbourne & Sydney, contact, booking CTA.

Prioritise the "Book & Pay Online" CTA and trust signals. Tight, confident copy. Fast and skimmable on a phone.
